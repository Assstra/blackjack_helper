### Preventive measures

- Get written consent from the owner of the system before conducting any penetration testing.
- Conduct testing within the scope of the agreement.
- Take measures to prevent damage to the system.
- Do not disclose any sensitive information obtained during the testing.
- Do not intercept electronic communications without proper authorization.
- Do not conduct testing on systems that are covered by the health insurance portability and accountability act (HIPAA) without proper authorization.
